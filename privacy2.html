Privacy Policy for Our Games

Effective date: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all games and mini-games developed, published, or operated by WEST CORPORATE SDN BHD (“we”, “us”, or “our”) through TikTok (collectively, the “Games”). It explains how information is handled when you use any of our Games.

1. Information handled by us

Our Games do not use a developer-operated backend. We do not directly collect or store your name, TikTok username, avatar, email address, telephone number, contacts, precise location, or payment information.

2. Silent login

Our Games may call TikTok's silent-login function as part of their platform integration. A Game may receive a temporary authorization code from TikTok. We do not send that code to a developer-operated backend, exchange it for an OpenID or access token, or store it. We do not request access to your TikTok profile, username, or avatar.

TikTok may process account, device, and technical information when providing its platform and login services. That processing is governed by TikTok's own privacy policy.

3. Local game data

Game progress, settings, and locally earned game rewards are stored only in local storage or cache on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers. Clearing a Game's cache, clearing app data, or uninstalling TikTok may permanently delete this local data. Because we do not hold a server copy, we cannot restore it.

4. Advertising

Our Games may display rewarded-video and interstitial advertisements supplied through TikTok's advertising services. TikTok and its advertising service providers may process information such as device and network information, IP address, advertising identifiers, ad impressions, ad clicks or interactions, diagnostic information, and anti-fraud information to deliver, measure, secure, and improve advertisements. We may receive aggregated advertising-performance reports that do not directly identify you.

For more information about TikTok's data practices and your advertising choices, please review the TikTok Privacy Policy and the privacy controls available in your TikTok account.

5. How information is used

Local game data is used only to operate the relevant Game on your device, remember settings, and preserve local progress. Advertising-related information processed by TikTok is used by TikTok and its providers for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

6. Sharing and sale of personal information

We do not sell personal information. Because we do not operate a backend or directly collect personal information, we do not disclose such information to third parties. Information independently processed by TikTok or its advertising service providers is subject to their respective policies.

7. Data retention and deletion

We do not retain personal information on our servers. Local game data remains on your device until you clear the relevant cache or app data, or uninstall the application. Retention by TikTok and its service providers is controlled by their policies and your TikTok account settings.

8. Children's privacy

Our Games are not directed specifically to children below the minimum age permitted to use TikTok in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided information to us may contact us using the details below.

9. Your rights and choices

You can delete local game data by clearing the relevant Game's cache or app data. You can manage TikTok privacy and advertising choices through your TikTok account settings. Depending on your location, you may also have rights under applicable privacy law. You may contact us about those rights, although we may have no server-held data with which to identify your game account.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our Games, platform services, or applicable requirements change. We will publish the revised version at this URL and update the effective date above.
