Privacy Policy for Our Facebook Games

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to games and mini-games developed, published, or operated by WEST CORPORATE SDN BHD ("we", "us", or "our") through Facebook Instant Games and related Facebook gaming surfaces provided by Meta (collectively, the "Games"). It explains how information is handled when you use these Games. It does not cover our games on TikTok or other platforms.

1. Information handled by us

Our Games do not use a developer-operated game backend or a separate developer-operated account system. During ordinary gameplay, we do not request or retrieve your Facebook player identifier, name, username, profile picture, email address, telephone number, contacts, friends list, precise location, or payment information. We do not ask for your Facebook password.

Game progress, settings, and locally earned rewards are handled on your device as described below. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, as explained in Section 5. Meta and its service providers may separately process information to provide the platform and advertisements; that processing is described in Sections 3 and 4.

2. Local game data

Game progress, settings, and locally earned rewards are stored in local storage or cache on your device to operate the relevant Game and preserve your preferences and progress. We do not upload this data to our own servers or use a platform cloud-save feature to synchronize it between devices.

Local data may remain until the relevant browser or application storage is cleared, or until the platform removes it. Changing devices or browsers may make existing progress unavailable. Because we do not hold a server backup, we cannot restore locally deleted data. Deletion instructions are provided in Section 8.

3. Facebook and Meta platform services

You access our Games through Facebook. Meta handles your Facebook account, platform authentication, and the platform environment in which the Games run. Our Games do not use TikTok's login services.

Meta may process account, device, network, technical, security, and activity information when providing Facebook and its gaming services. A platform identifier being available through an SDK does not mean our Games retrieve or retain it. Under the configuration described in this Policy, we do not retrieve a Facebook player identifier or create a player database.

Information processed independently by Meta is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy and applicable product notices. This Policy describes our Games and does not replace Meta's policies.

4. Advertising

Our Games may display rewarded-video and interstitial advertisements supplied through Facebook or Meta advertising services. Meta and its advertising service providers may process information such as device and network information, IP address, advertising or platform identifiers, ad impressions, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and fraud-prevention signals to deliver, measure, secure, and improve advertisements, subject to their policies, available choices, and applicable law.

The Game may receive a signal that an advertisement has completed so it can grant the advertised in-game reward. We may receive aggregated advertising-performance reports that do not directly identify individual players. We do not operate a separate advertising-profile database.

Use the privacy and advertising controls available through your Facebook settings or Meta Accounts Center to manage the choices available to you. For details about Meta's processing, see the Meta Privacy Policy.

5. Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive your email address, the content of your message, and any attachments or other information you voluntarily provide. We use this information to answer questions, resolve issues, process privacy requests, and keep records where reasonably necessary for those purposes or required by law. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, or unrelated sensitive information.

Our email service provider processes correspondence to deliver and store email on our behalf. Support information is accessible only as needed to handle your request or meet legal obligations. We retain it only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes, then delete it or remove identifying details.

6. Purposes and legal bases

We use local game data to provide the Game you choose to play, remember settings, and preserve local progress. We use support information to respond to you, resolve problems, and handle privacy requests. Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on performance of a contract for information necessary to provide a requested service, legitimate interests in responding to requests and protecting our services where those interests are not overridden by your rights, legal obligations, or consent where required. Where consent is required for particular device storage or advertising activities, those activities must be subject to the applicable consent controls.

Meta determines the legal bases for information it processes independently, as explained in its own policies.

7. Sharing, security, and international processing

We do not sell your personal information for money or use support correspondence to build advertising profiles. We may share support information with service providers acting on our behalf where needed to handle your request, or with authorities or other parties where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect legal rights. We do not upload your local game progress to those providers.

Platform advertising can involve processing or disclosure of information by Meta and its advertising providers as described in Section 4; the absence of a developer-operated game backend does not mean those services process no personal information.

We use reasonable measures to protect information under our control, although no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We are based in Malaysia, and support correspondence may be processed there or in other countries where our email providers operate. Where applicable law requires safeguards for transfers we make, we apply those safeguards. Meta's own international processing is described in its policies.

8. Data retention and deletion instructions

These instructions are available to every user of our Games.

A. Delete local game data

On a browser, use its site-data or storage settings to clear the storage associated with the Game. In a Facebook application, use the storage controls available for the relevant Game or application on your device. The exact controls depend on the browser, device, and Facebook version. Clearing cache alone may not remove persistent local storage. If no Game-specific control is available, contact us for help before clearing broader application or browser data, which may also affect other games or sign you out.

Clearing the relevant storage permanently removes the local progress stored there. We cannot erase storage on your device remotely or restore it after deletion.

B. Request deletion of information held by us

Email hanhuahe4@gmail.com with the subject "Facebook Game Data Deletion Request". Include the Game name and enough information to identify any previous correspondence you want deleted. You do not need to provide a Facebook password or identity document. We may request only additional information reasonably necessary to locate the information and verify your request.

We do not maintain a server-side player profile or game-progress database under this Policy. If we hold information from support correspondence or another request, we will delete information you ask us to delete as soon as reasonably possible, subject to applicable law and any lawful retention requirement. We will respond within the period required by applicable law. If we have no relevant information, we will explain that. We cannot delete data held independently by Meta.

C. Manage Facebook authorization and Meta-held data

Use the app and game controls available in your Facebook settings, such as Apps and Websites or the relevant gaming settings, to remove or manage a Game's access where that option is available. Removing access does not necessarily clear local storage or erase every record held by Meta. Use Facebook's privacy controls and the Meta Privacy Policy for requests concerning your Facebook account and information held by Meta.

A deletion request to us concerns information held by us; it does not delete your Facebook or Meta account.

9. Children's privacy

Our Games are not directed specifically to children below the minimum age permitted to use Facebook and the relevant Game in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information to us, they may contact us at the email address below. We will review the request and delete information where required.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

For information controlled independently by Meta, use the request and privacy controls provided by Meta. Your available advertising choices depend on your location, account, and applicable law.
